The First 90 Days After Your Raise

Turn your use-of-proceeds plan into a cash forecast, reporting cadence, and clear operating responsibilities.

By Centripetal Advisors Concept updated October 9, 2026

On this page

Connect the raise to an operating cadence.

Use the first 90 days as a planning horizon, adapting the sequence to your team and existing foundation. The goal is to connect the use-of-proceeds plan to cash, hiring, reporting, and decision ownership.

Weeks 1–2 · Cash access and control

Reconcile the opening balances, map operating and reserve cash, and confirm access and approval responsibilities. Verify the actual coverage and liquidity of the banking structure with your providers.

Weeks 3–4 · Reporting responsibilities

Agree the close calendar, board reporting dates, metric definitions, and owners. Use the same source data across the board package and operating model; explain any differences.

Weeks 5–8 · Forecast and scenarios

Connect hiring dates, collections, delivery costs, and payment commitments to the model. Compare operating scenarios and agree which conditions would trigger a hiring or spending decision.

Weeks 9–12 · A repeatable review

Review actuals against the plan, refresh assumptions, and maintain a decision log. Test whether the reporting and cash review can continue when the founder’s attention moves elsewhere.

Finish with a connected operating system.

A current model, a rolling cash view, a reporting calendar, named owners, and documented decision triggers should support the next stage together. Review the foundation with the SaaS Finance Scorecard.
