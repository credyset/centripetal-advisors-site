Fractional CFO or VP Finance? The Decision Is Complexity, Not ARR

When does daily operating ownership change the finance leadership model?

← Back to BlogConcept article · Content and factual review pending.

On this page

  • Compare continuity and responsibility
  • Make the recurring work visible
  • Use current proposals for the cost comparison

Compare continuity and responsibility

A fractional CFO engagement can provide senior finance support within an agreed scope. An internal finance leader may be more suitable when the company needs continuing daily management, execution, and team ownership. The comparison depends on the work and availability required, not a universal ARR or hours threshold.

Make the recurring work visible

Describe the close and reporting cadence, forecast maintenance, decision support, board preparation, and management of the finance team. Identify recurring duties separately from a temporary cleanup or financing project.

Multiple entities, contract complexity, reporting obligations, or cross-functional dependencies can change the work. They do not automatically determine which employment model fits. Ask how each option would cover routine responsibilities and periods of peak demand.

Use current proposals for the cost comparison

Compare the same responsibilities, experience, availability, and expected outputs. For an internal hire, consider compensation, benefits, recruitment, management, and any equity terms. For a provider, consider the agreed scope, additional work, response expectations, and transition arrangements. Obtain current proposals rather than relying on unsourced salary ranges.

Before selecting a structure, document what the founder will hand off and what a future hire should inherit: reporting calendar, models, assumptions, systems, and ownership. The wider guide below gives a practical framework for that brief.
