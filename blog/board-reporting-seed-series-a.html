Board Reporting for Seed and Series A Companies

Which decision should the board package support at this meeting?

← Back to BlogConcept article · Content and factual review pending.

On this page

  • Open with the decision and the request
  • Keep the evidence connected
  • Close with owners and the next review

Open with the decision and the request

A useful board package connects management’s recommendation to the operating evidence, alternatives, and requested input or decision. Adapt the structure and cadence to the board’s agreed agenda. A longer deck does not by itself make the decision clearer.

Keep the evidence connected

Explain what changed since the prior meeting, which assumptions moved, and which previous actions remain open. Keep recurring-revenue definitions, cash timing, and reporting cutoffs consistent across the package.

For the main discussion, identify the evidence needed to compare the options. A hiring proposal might need collections and payroll timing; a revenue discussion might need customer-level retention and implementation status. Include the relevant details rather than adding every available metric.

Close with owners and the next review

Record the discussion outcome, the action owner, the next check date, and the condition that would reopen the decision. Confirm formal approvals through the company’s actual governance process.

Illustrative example: contracted growth supports a hire, but the customer start date moves. Distinguish the signed contract from active recurring revenue and cash collection. Explain which commitment depends on the milestone and who will confirm it.
