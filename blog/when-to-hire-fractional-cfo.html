When to Hire a Fractional CFO

What changed in the company that creates a need for senior finance ownership?

The trigger is not ARR

Most advice frames the fractional CFO decision as a revenue milestone: hire one at $2M ARR, or after your Series A, or when you have 50 employees.

That framing is too simple. Companies at $1M ARR can have complex capital structures that require senior financial judgment. Companies at $8M ARR can have straightforward finance operations that a strong controller handles well. The trigger is the gap between the financial decisions the company faces and the financial capacity the team has.

Three signals that the gap exists

First: the founder is spending more than 10 hours a week on finance and is not good at it or does not enjoy it. That time has an opportunity cost.

Second: a board, an investor, or a lender is asking financial questions that the team cannot answer confidently without building a new spreadsheet.

Third: a capital event is approaching — a raise, a debt facility, a major contract negotiation — and the financial infrastructure underneath the company is not ready for the scrutiny.

What a fractional CFO actually does

An embedded fractional CFO is not a consultant who drops in monthly. At Centripetal, the model is 10-20 hours per week, fully embedded in the operating rhythm: cash planning, board reporting, capital decisions, scenario modeling, and the judgment calls that sit between finance and strategy.

The work is not bookkeeping. It is not tax preparation. It is the layer between the bookkeeper's output and the founder's decisions.

When to wait

If the highest-value work is processing transactions, reconciling accounts, and producing monthly close packages, the company needs a bookkeeper or controller — not a CFO. If the founder is handling finance comfortably and the board is not asking questions that stretch the team's capacity, the gap may not exist yet.

Frequently Asked Questions

At what revenue should a SaaS company hire a fractional CFO?
There is no universal revenue threshold. The trigger is the gap between the financial decisions the company faces and the financial capacity the current team has. Some companies need one at $1M ARR; others do not until much later.
What does a fractional CFO do differently than a bookkeeper?
A bookkeeper processes transactions and produces financial records. A fractional CFO provides senior financial judgment: cash planning, board reporting, capital strategy, scenario modeling, and the interpretation that connects financial data to operating decisions.
How many hours per week does a fractional CFO typically work?
Embedded fractional CFOs typically work 10-20 hours per week, integrated into the company's operating rhythm rather than visiting monthly.
