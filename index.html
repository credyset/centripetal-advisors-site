Your Financial
Co-Pilot

We combine strategic financial market knowledge and operational expertise, guiding SaaS founders to success. Our commitment is to provide the highest level of financial stewardship, allowing founders to focus on what they do best—innovate, grow, and deliver value to their customers.

Our clients benefit from having a partner who can:

  • Help raise capital (debt or equity) at the most favorable terms possible

  • Gaining key insights into how their business is performing against goals and industry benchmarks

  • Assurance that the finance and accounting functions are managed to meet investor standards, with necessary data accessible at all times

  • Scaling business operations through a data-driven approach

  • Never worrying about tax preparation ever again

  • Evaluating and executing M&A when appropriate

  • Partnering with a dedicated co-pilot who provides both professional and personal founder support

About Us

Charles Solomon

Founder/Managing Partner

Centripetal was founded in 2018 to support founders. Having met countless founders all over the world, we learned two things:


1. Finance & accounting are areas that frustrate and confuse founders.

2. The founder journey is not linear and often challenging and lonely.


We built a firm to address these two problems by providing fractional CFO expertise. We ensure founders never lose out economically due to lack of financial knowledge, and we minimize stress during their entrepreneurial journey as much as we can.
