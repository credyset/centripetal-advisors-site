How We Work With You

We don't consult from the sidelines. We embed into your team as your CFO and build the financial infrastructure your business needs to scale.

Core Engagement

Embedded Fractional CFO

We dedicate 10–20 hours per week to each client, with a maximum of 2–4 clients per CFO. This is not advisory work from a distance. We embed into your team, attend your leadership meetings, and operate as your CFO.

Not a consultant who drops in quarterly. Not a bookkeeper with a new title. A dedicated finance leader who knows your business cold and is accountable for results.

  • Dedicated weekly hours embedded with your leadership team
  • Direct participation in board meetings and investor calls
  • Full ownership of the finance function and financial strategy
  • Continuity and context that outside advisors cannot match
Reporting & Governance

Board & Investor Reporting

Your story should be told with data, not guesswork. We build the reporting infrastructure that keeps your board informed and your investors confident.

  • Monthly and quarterly board packages designed for clarity
  • Investor updates that highlight the metrics that matter
  • KPI dashboards with real-time visibility into business health
  • Narrative frameworks that connect financial data to strategy

When your next raise comes, the data room is already built.

Cash & Treasury

Cash & Treasury Management

Know where every dollar is and where it's going. We build the systems and policies that give you complete visibility into your cash position and the confidence to deploy capital strategically.

  • Cash flow forecasting with rolling 13-week and long-range models
  • Treasury policy development and implementation
  • Banking relationship management and optimization
  • Working capital optimization and scenario planning
Capital Markets

Fundraising & Capital Strategy

Capital strategy is not a one-time event. Whether you are raising your first institutional round or structuring venture debt alongside equity, we bring the financial rigor and process that gets deals closed.

  • Financial modeling built for investor-grade scrutiny
  • Pitch support and investor materials development
  • Due diligence preparation and data room construction
  • Term sheet analysis and negotiation support
Track record: $115M+ raised across 22+ transactions
