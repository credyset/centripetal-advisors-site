Cash Flow & Runway Planning

Cash flow planning shows when money is expected to enter and leave the business. Runway planning connects that timing to operating choices, so a founder can see how hiring, collections, and spending change the cash position.

Board & Investor Reporting

Board and investor reporting should explain performance, the assumptions behind the plan, and the decisions that need discussion. Centripetal connects the financial package to the operating context, rather than treating the deck as a separate task.
