Our Story

Built for SaaS Companies That Outgrow Their Financial Infrastructure

Centripetal Advisors was founded in 2018 by Charles Solomon on a straightforward belief: high-growth SaaS companies deserve the same caliber of financial leadership as public companies, without the overhead of a full-time executive hire.

Too many SaaS founders hit an inflection point where the complexity of their financial operations outpaces their existing resources. Fundraising, board reporting, cash management, capital structure decisions, revenue recognition -- these demands land on founders who should be focused on product and customers.

We built Centripetal to solve that problem. We embed into your organization as your CFO, not as outside consultants delivering slide decks. We attend your board meetings, manage your investor relationships, own your financial model, and operate as a member of your leadership team.
