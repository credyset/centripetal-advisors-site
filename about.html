Our story

Built around
the founder.

Centripetal was founded to address two connected challenges: the demands of finance and accounting, and the uncertainty that comes with building a company.

Strong financial support should help a founder understand the choices in front of them, connect those choices to the numbers, and keep the finance function moving. It also means having a consistent partner through an entrepreneurial journey that rarely follows a straight line.

That is the role of a financial co-pilot: bringing financial stewardship into the operating conversation so founders can keep their attention on the company they are building.

Founder & Managing Partner

Charles Solomon

Charles founded Centripetal Advisors in 2018 to support founders through the financial and personal demands of growing a company. The firm combines strategic financial market knowledge with operational expertise, connecting capital decisions to the work of running the business.

The focus is ongoing financial stewardship: helping founders prepare for financing, understand business performance, and build a finance function that can support investor expectations.
