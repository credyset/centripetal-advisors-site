Board & Investor Reporting

Board and investor reporting should explain performance, the assumptions behind the plan, and the decisions that need discussion. Centripetal connects the financial package to the operating context, rather than treating the deck as a separate task.

When the discussion starts after the slides end

The useful questions are often about what changed: why expenses moved, whether a hiring plan still makes sense, or how customer concentration affects the forecast. The reporting package needs evidence and interpretation behind the headline metrics.

What we help you own

A consistent financial view

Bring financial statements, cash forecasts, budgets, and operating metrics into a reporting cadence with clear definitions.

Decision-focused interpretation

Explain the material variances, distinguish timing from performance, and identify what management is changing in response.

Continuity with investors

Maintain the supporting schedules and investor updates so board reporting and a future diligence process draw from the same foundation.

What the work produces

The output can include a board financial package, budget-to-actual analysis, a refreshed forecast, KPI definitions, and investor updates. We agree the reporting scope and cadence around your company and board.

How we begin

We start with a conversation about your company, the immediate decisions, and who owns finance today. From there, we assess the current foundation and agree on priorities, scope, and a working cadence.

Questions founders ask

Do you help prepare for the board conversation?
Yes. Reporting support includes connecting the numbers to the operating narrative and identifying the assumptions, variances, and decisions that deserve discussion. The work should help the leadership team explain the business confidently.
Can this be part of an embedded CFO engagement?
Yes. These responsibilities connect to the broader finance function. The engagement scope is agreed around your company’s needs and can evolve as the business grows.
