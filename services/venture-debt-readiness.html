Venture Debt Readiness

Evaluate a debt facility within the full capital plan. Centripetal supports lender preparation and financing-process work, connecting proposed terms, repayment, and draw timing to the company’s operating and cash scenarios.

Before additional capital becomes an additional obligation

Available capital is only one part of the decision. Draw timing, fees, repayment, contractual conditions, and the company’s downside cash path can change how useful a facility is for the intended plan.

We start with the use of capital and the operating assumptions. The work connects lender questions to financial records and compares the proposed obligations with the company’s funding path, alongside counsel’s review of the agreement.

The work we help you own

Examine the financing fit

Connect the facility to specific uses of capital and milestones. Compare the expected funding path with slower collections, delayed financing, or changes in the operating plan.

Prepare the lender information

Organize financial statements, customer revenue schedules, forecasts, and the supporting evidence behind lender questions. Track open items and responsible owners.

Model the proposed terms

Connect draw dates, interest, fees, repayment, and relevant contractual conditions to the cash forecast. Compare alternatives using explicit assumptions.

Support the process and follow-through

Coordinate financial requests and questions through the debt process. Connect agreed reporting and repayment responsibilities to the ongoing finance cadence.

Working outputs

These are practical materials the work can produce. We agree priorities and deliverables around your company and the engagement scope.

Lender information package
Financials, revenue and customer schedules, forecasts, and a tracker of open lender questions.
Financing and repayment comparison
Cash scenarios reflecting the proposed draw schedule, payments, fees, and relevant conditions.
Obligation and reporting calendar
Named owners and dates for the financial reporting, cash review, and follow-up work within the agreed scope.

A decision this work can clarify

Does the facility still fit if the next raise is delayed?

Working inputs
Proposed terms, draw timing, repayment, current cash, and the operating funding plan.
The analysis
Compare the expected case with a delayed-financing cash scenario and identify the conditions needing further review.
A useful output
A financing comparison and a specific question list for management, the lender, and counsel.

How we begin

  1. Clarify why debt is being considered, how the capital would be used, and whether lender discussions or proposed terms already exist.
  2. Review the cash forecast, capital plan, financial records, and customer schedules against the likely lender questions.
  3. Agree the preparation and process scope, including financial modeling, specialist review, and responsibility for follow-up requests.

Questions founders ask

Can we start before receiving a term sheet?
Yes. The first step can be clarifying the use of capital and organizing the information behind lender questions. Specific financing comparisons become more useful once proposed terms and conditions are available.
Does your role stop at a lender introduction?
The engagement can include financial preparation, lender questions, financing scenarios, and process coordination. Responsibilities are agreed around the company and transaction, including the work after a facility is put in place.
Can debt replace an equity raise?
That depends on the operating plan and proposed terms. Debt introduces repayment and contractual obligations; the comparison should account for those obligations and the company’s capital needs rather than treating the two sources as interchangeable.
Who reviews the covenants and financing agreement?
Counsel reviews the legal agreement. Centripetal models the financial implications and scenarios, and coordinates the questions requiring management, lender, or specialist input.
