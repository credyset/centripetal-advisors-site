When growth makes informal processes fragile

A payment process can work until the approver is unavailable. A close can finish until a missing handoff delays the model and board package. The issue is often unclear ownership across banking, billing, collections, payroll, bookkeeping, and specialist work.

We connect these responsibilities to the decisions they support. The goal is a finance process the team can maintain, with documented approvals, useful records, and a clear path for resolving exceptions.

The work we help you own

Clarify cash access and approvals

Review banking access, payment approval roles, changes to payment details, and independent review. Connect treasury decisions and liquidity needs to the cash forecast.

Build a reliable accounting foundation

Review the SaaS chart of accounts, reconciliations, close responsibilities, and reporting inputs in QuickBooks or Xero. Prioritize improvements that fit the company’s stage.

Coordinate the back office

Make the handoffs across billing, collections, payroll, bookkeeping, tax preparation, and tax-credit specialists explicit. Help assess vendors and define who owns recurring deadlines.

Document the review process

Establish a close calendar, exception handling, and review responsibilities. Keep source records and the operating model connected as the team changes.

How we begin

  1. Identify the cash-control, close, or back-office handoff creating the most pressure today.
  2. Map the accounts, accounting system, vendors, recurring deadlines, and approval responsibilities behind that issue.
  3. Agree the priority changes and which work belongs to your team, Centripetal, and specialist providers.

Questions founders ask

Do you replace our bookkeeper or tax advisor?
We can work with existing specialists and clarify how their responsibilities connect to reporting and financial strategy. Vendor selection and handoff support can be included when the current setup needs to change.
Does improving finance require a new ERP?
The first step is to review the work, chart of accounts, source records, and ownership. Centripetal works with QuickBooks and Xero for companies at this stage; a software migration is not assumed to be the answer.
Can you coordinate tax and tax-credit work?
Coordination with the relevant specialists can be part of the finance function, including deadlines, records, and responsibilities. The scope and specialist roles should be clear; the service does not promise a particular tax-credit outcome.
How do treasury controls relate to runway planning?
Cash placement, liquidity, access, and payment timing affect the cash view the team uses. The processes should connect to the forecast and operating commitments rather than being reviewed in isolation.
