Treasury & Finance Operations

Treasury and finance operations give the business a dependable way to manage cash, approvals, records, and recurring finance work. Centripetal coordinates the people, systems, and review processes that keep those responsibilities owned.

When growth makes informal processes fragile

The same person approving a payment, changing banking details, and reconciling the transaction leaves little room for independent review. Growth also makes unclear ownership across close, payroll, billing, and tax coordination more costly.

What we help you own

Cash and banking processes

Review banking access, approval responsibilities, payment controls, and the connection between treasury decisions and the cash forecast.

A reliable back office

Coordinate bookkeeping, monthly reporting, billing and collections, payroll responsibilities, and tax work with the relevant specialists.

Systems that fit the stage

Review the accounting and finance stack, clarify source systems, and prioritize useful integrations and documented handoffs.

What the work produces

The working outputs may include a responsibility map, treasury policy, approval workflow, close calendar, and systems improvement plan. The aim is a process the team can maintain as the business changes.

How we begin

We start with a conversation about your company, the immediate decisions, and who owns finance today. From there, we assess the current foundation and agree on priorities, scope, and a working cadence.

Questions founders ask

Do you replace our bookkeeper or tax advisor?
The role is to coordinate the finance function and financial strategy. Centripetal can work with existing bookkeeping and tax specialists, clarify responsibilities, and help make their work part of a consistent operating process.
Can this be part of an embedded CFO engagement?
Yes. These responsibilities connect to the broader finance function. The engagement scope is agreed around your company’s needs and can evolve as the business grows.
