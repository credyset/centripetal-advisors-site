Fundraising Readiness

Fundraising readiness means being able to explain your operating performance, capital needs, and financial assumptions with supporting evidence. Centripetal helps build and maintain that evidence alongside the broader capital strategy.

Before a raise becomes a deadline

The work starts with the business: what milestones the capital should fund, how the operating plan reaches them, and where the assumptions could break. Financial preparation supports the raise; it does not replace customer traction or guarantee financing.

What we help you own

Model the use of proceeds

Connect hiring, sales capacity, collections, and spend to the milestones behind the proposed raise. Make downside assumptions explicit.

Prepare the financial diligence

Organize historical financials, customer revenue schedules, ownership information, and supporting documents with named owners.

Support capital decisions

Compare financing scenarios and model their effect on the company, with support for investor materials and term-sheet discussions alongside your legal advisors.

What the work produces

A working package can include the operating model, use-of-proceeds scenarios, data-room inventory, financial schedules, and a list of open diligence issues. The same underlying information should support your board reporting after the raise.

How we begin

We start with a conversation about your company, the immediate decisions, and who owns finance today. From there, we assess the current foundation and agree on priorities, scope, and a working cadence.

Questions founders ask

Can a financial model make us investor-ready?
A model is one part of preparation. Investors also evaluate the business, team, customer traction, and supporting evidence. The model should make your assumptions and capital requirements understandable; it cannot guarantee an investment.
Can this be part of an embedded CFO engagement?
Yes. These responsibilities connect to the broader finance function. The engagement scope is agreed around your company’s needs and can evolve as the business grows.
