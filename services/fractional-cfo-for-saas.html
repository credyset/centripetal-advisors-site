When financial complexity outgrows the team

A founder can have a bookkeeper, a budget, and monthly reports while still carrying every financial decision personally. The gap becomes visible when a board asks for a revised plan, hiring changes the cash outlook, or an investor needs evidence the team cannot assemble quickly.

The fit is about the work and its ownership. Centripetal focuses on Seed through Series A B2B SaaS companies, with priorities agreed around the company’s stage, existing team, and upcoming decisions.

The work we help you own

Connect strategy to the operating plan

Maintain the model behind revenue, spending, hiring, and capital needs. Work with the leadership team to explain assumptions and compare choices before they become commitments.

Coordinate the finance function

Clarify ownership across bookkeeping, close, billing, collections, payroll, and specialist tax work. Review the accounting setup and handoffs so reporting is supported by reliable records.

Own the reporting conversation

Connect management reporting, board materials, and investor updates to the same underlying information. Explain variances and the decisions that need attention.

Support capital and transitions

Coordinate financing preparation and capital decisions, then maintain the operating cadence after the transaction. Support a documented transition when the company hires an internal finance leader.

How we begin

  1. Start with the business goals, current finance owners, and upcoming decisions. Centripetal’s client diagnostic examines the underlying records and operating practices across eight finance areas.
  2. Review the books, operating model, cash view, and reporting handoffs to identify where information or ownership breaks down.
  3. Agree the engagement scope, priorities, review cadence, and responsibilities with your existing team.

Questions founders ask

How do you work with an existing bookkeeper or controller?
Bookkeeping and controllership provide the transaction records and close process. Centripetal coordinates that work with forecasting, capital decisions, board reporting, and the broader finance function. The engagement should make ownership clearer for everyone involved.
Can we bring you in before a fundraise?
Yes. An approaching raise can make the gaps urgent, but the model, financial schedules, and operating responsibilities need to work before investor requests accelerate. Preparation can be part of an ongoing CFO engagement.
What changes when we hire an internal finance leader?
We can help define the responsibilities and handoff, with documented processes and a maintained model. For many early-stage companies the next internal hire is a VP Finance or finance leader; the right role depends on the work the company needs.
Are these separate service packages?
The service areas describe connected responsibilities within the finance function. We agree which work belongs in your engagement rather than assuming every company needs the same package.
