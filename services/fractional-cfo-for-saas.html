Fractional CFO for SaaS

A fractional CFO gives your company senior financial leadership on a part-time basis. At Centripetal, that means an embedded financial co-pilot who connects the finance function to the decisions your leadership team is making.

When financial complexity outgrows the team

A fundraise, a changing hiring plan, or a more demanding board can expose a gap between having financial reports and having someone accountable for what they mean. The decision is about the work and judgment required, rather than a single revenue threshold.

What we help you own

Financial strategy

Connect the operating plan, budget, cash forecast, and capital requirements so decisions share the same assumptions.

An owned finance function

Coordinate bookkeeping, close, reporting, back-office responsibilities, and the systems that support them.

A partner through inflection points

Bring continuity to board meetings, investor conversations, fundraising preparation, and the eventual transition to an internal finance leader.

What the work produces

The practical outputs depend on the engagement: a financial diagnostic and priority plan, a maintained operating model, cash forecasts, management reporting, and a regular review cadence. We agree the scope and owners with you before work begins.

How we begin

We start with a conversation about your company, the immediate decisions, and who owns finance today. From there, we assess the current foundation and agree on priorities, scope, and a working cadence.

Questions founders ask

How is this different from bookkeeping?
Bookkeeping records the transactions. A CFO connects those records to cash decisions, operating trade-offs, financing, and board expectations. Centripetal works with the people maintaining your books and coordinates the broader finance function.
Can this be part of an embedded CFO engagement?
Yes. These responsibilities connect to the broader finance function. The engagement scope is agreed around your company’s needs and can evolve as the business grows.
